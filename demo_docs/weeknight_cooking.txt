Set a prep station on the left and a plating station on the right so dishes
move in one direction. Keep a master base of aromatics—onion, garlic, chili
paste—in a jar and fry a spoonful to start most meals. Double sauces on
Mondays so you can reuse them on Thursdays. Finish every dish with acid,
crunch, or herbs to make it feel fresh even if the protein repeats.
